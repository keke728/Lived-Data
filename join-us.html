Join the collective

We welcome students, collaborators, and researchers interested in exploring new ways of working with data.

For

Students

We welcome undergraduate and graduate students interested in visualization, accessibility, HCI, storytelling, design, and creative technology.

For

Collaborators

We welcome artists, storytellers, designers, engineers, educators, community partners, and researchers interested in building more thoughtful and human-centered approaches to data.

What we value

Curiosity, Creativity, and Care

We value curiosity, creativity, care, and a willingness to experiment. You do not need to come from a single discipline to work with us.
